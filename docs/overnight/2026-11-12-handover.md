## Remaining defects (all low, all tester-only unless stated)

Quote-checked feedback, `src/lib/feedback/grounded-output.ts`:
1. Very unusual quotation characters (for example ｢ ｣ or ʻ ʻ) are not treated as quotations, so words inside them are not checked. An English model is very unlikely to produce them.
2. A quotation placed inside a heading is checked against everything you said, not against the turn it cites.
3. Any line containing a plural possessive ("students' views") is dropped, even when its quotation is correct. Safe, but it loses valid feedback and makes the fallback more likely.
4. A quotation wrapped over three lines can leave its middle line behind without quote marks.
5. Ordinary phrases such as "the river turns 3 times" are read as turn references, which can drop that sentence. Safe.

Word limit, `src/lib/feedback/word-limit.ts` (affects students too):
6. If a single heading were longer than the whole limit it would be cut mid-heading. Cannot realistically happen at 400 words.

Geography drafts: `hopeful-and-angry` and `hazards-city-volcano` each carry one optional prompt beyond the four push-on lines.
